# Visual review of the demonstrative head

Reviewed actual Blender clay front, three-quarter and profile renders and the textured mesh in the browser. Cranial silhouette, jaw projection, nose depth, cheek volume and ear attachment are visible in the different views. These observations are limited visual checks, not anatomical or biometric certification.

The supplied scan has closed eyes and no separate eyeball geometry. Lower shoulders were removed; the neck boundary remains open. Original UV0 is preserved on the face. Color and normal maps come from the licensed scan; roughness is a constant preview choice. No original portrait sculpt, photogrammetry capture, retopology or texture bake is claimed.

G3 remains REVIEW_REQUIRED: no independent subject landmarks or matched reference photographs are available. Structural GLB PASS is reported separately from visual quality and likeness acceptance.

Browser inspection: skin/clay/wireframe selection, side and three-quarter cameras, keyboard ArrowRight/+/Home, auto-rotate toggle, mobile 390px layout and desktop rendering. All buttons measured at least 44px tall. No horizontal overflow or broken gallery images observed. Initial viewer syntax and narrow-aspect camera-fit issues were corrected before this review.
